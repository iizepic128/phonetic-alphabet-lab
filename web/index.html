What is the MAPO alphabet?

Original coder mapo / mapoware trained a speech recognition model to explore alternatives to the NATO spelling alphabet. This app uses the best-recognized and most-confusing word lists saved in the project’s analysis notebook. These are experimental results from one training run and may differ from the video. The app converts text using those saved lists; it does not run or retrain the model.

Spaces separate word groups. Numbers and punctuation are kept as typed. Speech uses your browser’s available English voice; support and pronunciation vary.
